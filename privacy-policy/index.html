Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Introduction

This policy explains how Joe McHugh, trading as Só Coaching, collects and uses personal information when you visit socoaching.ie, contact him, subscribe to his newsletter, arrange an introductory conversation or purchase a book. Só Coaching is based in Ireland and processes personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Irish data protection law.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Joe McHugh, trading as Só Coaching, Dún Mór, Carraig Airt, Leitir Ceanainn, F92 XOH4, Ireland. Email: joe@socoaching.ie.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a contact form, we receive the name, email address and any information you include in your message. Form submissions are handled using Formspree. When you email us, we receive your email address and correspondence. If you click to book a conversation, you leave this website and provide booking information directly through Cal.com, which operates under its own privacy notice. We may receive your booking details and related correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry unless necessary.

When you purchase a book, we receive your name, email address, delivery address and order details. Payments are processed through Stripe. We receive payment confirmation and transaction details but do not receive or store your full payment card details.

We need your contact and delivery details to fulfil your order.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and subscription status through Kit. Kit also processes technical information associated with the signup, such as browser and IP information.

3. How we use information

If you choose to subscribe, we use your email address to send Joe’s stories, reflections, posts and updates and to manage your subscription. You can subscribe using the newsletter form or the optional, unticked checkbox on the contact form. If you tick that box, your newsletter choice is included with your enquiry and, after your message is successfully sent, your email address is submitted separately to Kit. Your message and coaching interests are not sent to Kit. Leaving the checkbox unticked does not affect your enquiry.

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange conversations, discuss potential coaching services, provide agreed services and maintain relevant business records. We do not use enquiry details for unsolicited marketing or sell personal information.

When you purchase a book, we use your information to process and fulfil your order, arrange delivery, communicate with you about your purchase and handle any returns or refunds.

4. Legal basis

We send newsletters with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting joe@socoaching.ie. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

For enquiries and bookings about coaching, processing may be necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For general correspondence and reasonable administration of the business, we may rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), taking account of your rights. Where information must be retained to meet a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c) applies. Any separate processing of sensitive personal data requires an appropriate additional legal condition.

For book purchases, we process personal information as necessary to fulfil the purchase contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and to comply with applicable legal and accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

5. How long we keep information

Newsletter contact details are used while you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop newsletter emails and may retain a limited record of your opt-out so that your choice can be respected.

Enquiries and related correspondence are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up where requested and provide any agreed services. We periodically review and delete information that is no longer needed, subject to legal, accounting or legitimate record-keeping requirements. Booking records are reviewed on the same basis.

Book order records are retained for as long as necessary to fulfil orders and manage returns or refunds. Records required for tax and accounting purposes are generally retained for six years, or longer where legally required.

6. Service providers and sharing

We use Cloudflare Pages to host the website, Formspree to process contact forms, our email service provider for correspondence, Kit for newsletter subscriptions and delivery, and Cal.com for external bookings. The website also displays a LinkedIn feed supplied by SociableKIT. These services may receive information necessary to provide their functions. We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing. We may disclose information where legally required.

We use Stripe to process payments for book purchases. Stripe collects and processes payment information in accordance with its own privacy practices. We may also share the information necessary for delivery with postal or courier services. For more information, see Stripe’s Privacy Policy.

7. LinkedIn feed

Our Friday Corner section displays public LinkedIn posts through SociableKIT. Loading the widget may involve technical information being sent to SociableKIT, such as an IP address or browser information. If you follow a link to LinkedIn, LinkedIn’s own privacy and cookie policies apply. See our Cookie Policy for details about website storage technologies.

8. International transfers

Some of our service providers process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where required by GDPR, transfers must be covered by an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict processing, object to certain processing and request data portability where applicable. You may complain to Ireland’s Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie. To make a request, email joe@socoaching.ie.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information, including restricting access and using established hosting, form and email providers. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children

The website and coaching services are directed at adult professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

12. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the website and services change. The date above indicates the latest revision. For questions, contact Joe McHugh, Só Coaching, at joe@socoaching.ie.
