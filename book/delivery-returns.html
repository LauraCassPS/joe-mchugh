Delivery & Returns

Delivery

We deliver within Ireland and to international destinations available at checkout. Postage costs depend on the destination and are shown before you complete your purchase.

Dispatch: Orders are normally posted within three working days.

Estimated delivery after dispatch:

  • Ireland: Normally the next working day.
  • International: Normally 3–7 working days, depending on the destination.

These estimates are based on An Post Standard Post. Delivery times are not guaranteed. International deliveries may take longer because of customs clearance or local postal delays.

International orders may be subject to customs duties or import charges, payable by the recipient where applicable.

For delivery enquiries, email joe@socoaching.ie.

Cancellations and returns

You may cancel your order without giving a reason within 14 days of receiving your book.

To cancel, email joe@socoaching.ie, clearly stating that you wish to cancel. Please include your order number, if available, to help us locate your purchase.

You may use our cancellation form, but you do not have to. Any clear statement of your decision to cancel is sufficient.

You then have a further 14 days to send the book back.

Unless the book is faulty or incorrectly supplied, you are responsible for return postage costs.

We will refund the price paid and the cost of our standard outward delivery service. If you chose a more expensive delivery option, the additional cost will not be refunded.

Refunds will be made using your original payment method, without undue delay and within 14 days of being informed of your cancellation. We may withhold the refund until we receive the returned book or you provide evidence that you have sent it back, whichever happens first.

You may inspect the book as you would in a shop. Any reduction in value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to inspect it may be deducted from your refund.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect books

If your book arrives damaged, faulty or incorrect, please email joe@socoaching.ie with your order number and details of the problem so we can arrange an appropriate remedy in accordance with your consumer rights.

Your statutory rights are not affected.
