Professional standards

Code of Ethics & Professional Conduct

Trust is central to a good coaching relationship. You should feel confident about how I work, how your information is treated and the professional standards that guide our conversations.

My practice is guided by the Global Code of Ethics for Coaches, Mentors and Supervisors. The commitments below explain what this means for our work together.

Integrity

I work with honesty, fairness and care. I represent my experience, qualifications and approach accurately, and I am clear about what coaching can offer.

I will discuss any personal or professional interests that could affect our work together. If a conflict cannot be managed appropriately, we will agree how to bring the coaching relationship to a close.

Confidentiality

I treat what you share with me as confidential, both during our work together and after it ends.

Before coaching begins, we will agree the boundaries of confidentiality, including circumstances where information may need to be disclosed because of legal requirements or a risk of harm to you or others.

If your organisation is funding the coaching, we will agree at the outset what, if anything, will be shared with them. For team coaching, we will also agree expectations for confidentiality between participants and any reporting to the organisation.

I handle coaching records and communications carefully, with respect for your privacy and applicable data-protection requirements.

Respect

I respect your background, identity, beliefs and perspective. I aim to create a space where you can speak openly, reflect honestly and explore different possibilities without discrimination.

Our work will support your independence and your ability to make your own decisions.

Clear agreements and professional boundaries

Before we begin, we will agree the purpose of the coaching, how we will work together, fees and practical arrangements.

I work within the limits of my training and experience. Coaching does not replace counselling, psychotherapy or medical care. If another form of support would better meet your needs, I will discuss this with you.

You may end the coaching relationship in accordance with the terms of our agreement.

Continuing professional development

I continue to develop my coaching practice through learning, reflection and professional supervision.

In supervision, I may discuss aspects of my work anonymously to reflect on my practice and maintain professional standards. The supervision relationship is itself confidential.

Questions or concerns

You are welcome to raise questions or concerns about our work together. I will take them seriously and respond respectfully.

Before we begin, I will explain the relevant professional complaints procedure and how you can access it.

About this statement

This is a summary of my commitments to clients. It does not replace the full Global Code of Ethics for Coaches, Mentors and Supervisors.
