Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. About cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files saved to a device. Similar technologies include local storage, which can also be subject to Irish rules on storing or accessing information on a visitor’s device.

2. How this website works

Só Coaching does not use website analytics or advertising cookies. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and its contact form uses Formspree with spam protection. Booking links open Cal.com in a separate website. The LinkedIn feed is provided by SociableKIT, as explained below.

3. LinkedIn feed

The Friday Corner section uses SociableKIT to display Joe’s public LinkedIn posts. SociableKIT has told us that this LinkedIn feed widget does not use cookies. SociableKIT may receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when the feed loads. Links to LinkedIn open LinkedIn’s website, where LinkedIn’s own cookie policy applies.

4. Your choices

You can manage or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. If non-essential cookies or similar storage are introduced on this website, we will ask for consent before using them and update this policy.

5. External websites

Links to LinkedIn and Cal.com take you to other websites, which have their own privacy and cookie notices.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the technologies used on the site change. Questions can be sent to joemchugh.coaching@gmail.com.
